GradeMate! is an intelligent chatbot that lets students check their grades, get academic FAQs answered, and receive academic-risk alerts — without waiting in line at the registrar's window.

Log in to GradeMate! See how it works
01 · Core Feature

Conversational Chatbot

Ask GradeMate! about your grades, requirements, deadlines, or the grading system, and get an instant, conversational answer — in English or Filipino.

02 · Key Module

Grade & Performance Lookup

A read-only, always-current view of every subject's quarterly grade, pulled straight from your student record.

03 · For Advisers

Academic Risk Alerts

Grades below the passing mark automatically flag a student for review, so struggling students get help before it's too late.
